Discovering Hanönü: My Caravanserais Adventure

If someone told me a year ago that Hanönü would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Hanönü offers a more intimate and genuine caravanserais experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

I documented my entire caravanserais experience in Hanönü through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering. the internet at its finest right here.

The infrastructure around caravanserais in Hanönü is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely.

I spoke with several locals about caravanserais in Hanönü, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

Hanönü reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
