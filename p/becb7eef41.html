Discovering Köprüköy: My Caravanserais Adventure

If someone told me a year ago that Köprüköy would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Köprüköy offers a more intimate and genuine caravanserais experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

I documented my entire caravanserais experience in Köprüköy through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

If you are interested in caravanserais, Köprüköy should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

What makes Köprüköy special when it comes to caravanserais is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

As I left Köprüköy, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
