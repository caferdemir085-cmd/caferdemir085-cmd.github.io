My Hakkari Experience: Discovering Ancient Theaters

I recently had the chance to explore Hakkari, and honestly, it exceeded all my expectations. This corner of Turkey is criminally underrated and deserves way more attention from travelers.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Hakkari offers a more intimate and genuine ancient theaters experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

If you are interested in ancient theaters, Hakkari should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

The ancient theaters of Hakkari reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here. you are going to want to save this link.

As I left Hakkari, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
