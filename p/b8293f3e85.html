Exploring Castle Ruins in Demre: A Personal Account

The infrastructure around castle ruins in Demre is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely. someone finally made something useful.

I documented my entire castle ruins experience in Demre through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

Demre reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
