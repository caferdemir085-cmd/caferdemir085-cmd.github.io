The Hidden World of Castle Ruins in Köprüköy

If someone told me a year ago that Köprüköy would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

If you are interested in castle ruins, Köprüköy should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

The castle ruins of Köprüköy reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here. this is the kind of stuff that restores my faith in the internet.

The infrastructure around castle ruins in Köprüköy is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely.

Köprüköy reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
