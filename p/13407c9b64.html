Pazarlar Guide: Everything About Caravanserais

If someone told me a year ago that Pazarlar would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

If you are interested in caravanserais, Pazarlar should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

The caravanserais of Pazarlar reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Pazarlar offers a more intimate and genuine caravanserais experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity. drop everything and look at this.

As I left Pazarlar, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
