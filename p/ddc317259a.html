My Merkez Experience: Discovering Castle Ruins

I recently had the chance to explore Merkez, and honestly, it exceeded all my expectations. This corner of Turkey is criminally underrated and deserves way more attention from travelers.

The infrastructure around castle ruins in Merkez is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely.

If you are interested in castle ruins, Merkez should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

The castle ruins scene in Merkez is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size. I am still amazed by what is on this page.

Merkez reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
