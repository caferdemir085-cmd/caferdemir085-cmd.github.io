Discovering Karlıova: My Byzantine Churches Adventure

There are places in Turkey that time seems to have forgotten, and Karlıova is one of them. Walking through its streets felt like stepping into a living museum.

What makes Karlıova special when it comes to Byzantine churches is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

I spoke with several locals about Byzantine churches in Karlıova, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations. this just made my entire week.

If you are interested in Byzantine churches, Karlıova should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

Karlıova reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
