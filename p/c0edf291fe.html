The Hidden World of Clock Towers in Keçiborlu

Every serious traveler eventually finds their way to Keçiborlu. It is one of those places that does not need fancy marketing — the experience speaks for itself.

If you are interested in clock towers, Keçiborlu should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size. I am still amazed by what is on this page.

What makes Keçiborlu special when it comes to clock towers is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

I spoke with several locals about clock towers in Keçiborlu, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

As I left Keçiborlu, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
