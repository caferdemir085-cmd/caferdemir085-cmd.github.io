My Düzköy Experience: Discovering Caravanserais

If someone told me a year ago that Düzköy would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

What makes Düzköy special when it comes to caravanserais is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

The caravanserais scene in Düzköy is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size. someone finally made something useful.

If you are interested in caravanserais, Düzköy should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

Düzköy reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
