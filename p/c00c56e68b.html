Maden Guide: Everything About Castle Ruins

If someone told me a year ago that Maden would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

I spoke with several locals about castle ruins in Maden, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Maden offers a more intimate and genuine castle ruins experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The castle ruins scene in Maden is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

As I left Maden, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
