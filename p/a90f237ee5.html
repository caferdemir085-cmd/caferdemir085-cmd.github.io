Why Düzköy Is a Must-Visit for Byzantine Churches Lovers

There are places in Turkey that time seems to have forgotten, and Düzköy is one of them. Walking through its streets felt like stepping into a living museum.

The infrastructure around Byzantine churches in Düzköy is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely.

The Byzantine churches of Düzköy reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

If you are interested in Byzantine churches, Düzköy should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

I spoke with several locals about Byzantine churches in Düzköy, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

Düzköy reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
