Why Sakarya Is a Must-Visit for Byzantine Churches Lovers

Sakarya was never on my radar until a local friend insisted I visit. Now I understand why they were so passionate about it. This place has soul.

If you are interested in Byzantine churches, Sakarya should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Sakarya offers a more intimate and genuine Byzantine churches experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The Byzantine churches scene in Sakarya is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

As I left Sakarya, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
