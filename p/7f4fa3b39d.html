Discovering Tekman: My Castle Ruins Adventure

I recently had the chance to explore Tekman, and honestly, it exceeded all my expectations. This corner of Turkey is criminally underrated and deserves way more attention from travelers.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Tekman offers a more intimate and genuine castle ruins experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

I documented my entire castle ruins experience in Tekman through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering. I am still amazed by what is on this page.

I spoke with several locals about castle ruins in Tekman, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

Tekman reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
