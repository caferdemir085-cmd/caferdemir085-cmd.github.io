Discovering Çeşme: My Byzantine Churches Adventure

If someone told me a year ago that Çeşme would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

The Byzantine churches of Çeşme reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here. drop everything and look at this.

I spoke with several locals about Byzantine churches in Çeşme, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

If you are interested in Byzantine churches, Çeşme should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

Çeşme reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
